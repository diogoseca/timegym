# TimeGym v2 publication strategy

Updated 28 October 2026; citation identifiers refreshed 30 October 2026. The author chose a new v2 manuscript for the redesigned package. No submission, arXiv replacement, or public release has been performed.

The paper's central contribution is an executable verification workflow: specify forecast semantics, separate invalid outputs from capability gaps, evaluate conditional references or declared relations, and reproduce a failure before checking a known repair. The mathematical references, notebook-authored software, and controlled studies support that account. Synthetic generators, unit tests, proper scores, and metamorphic testing are established ingredients. Preserve the 2021 source as historical material and attribute each new component to the current version.

For arXiv, a new manuscript does not necessarily mean a new identifier. The present scope continues and corrects the original TimeGym work, so **a substantial replacement of [arXiv:2105.01404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01404v1) is the provisional recommendation**. arXiv ordinarily versions corrections and expanded works under the same identifier, and may combine conceptually similar submissions even without substantial verbatim overlap. A separate, explicitly linked sequel is appropriate only if the final work is scientifically distinct; if the boundary remains ambiguous, ask arXiv support before submitting. This is an interpretation of the policy for this project, not an arXiv decision. [Official version policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/versions.html), [submission guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html).

A replacement receives its own date and version number; earlier versions remain accessible, and the unversioned identifier displays the latest version. It does not make new experiments a 2021 contribution. Explain the corrected reference semantics, redesigned software, and new experiments in the replacement comments and manuscript. [Official replacement guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/replace.html), [version availability](https://info.arxiv.org/help/versions.html).

There is no evidence here that retaining a 2021 identifier improves ingestion by AI systems or increases citations. No reviewed policy promises either outcome. The practical reason to retain the identifier is continuity for readers of the existing work. Use an accurate title, accessible abstract, checked references, a working package, reproducible experiments, and consistent links between the paper and repository. These make the work easier to inspect and cite; they are not a guarantee of search ranking, training inclusion, or citations.

The strongest current framing is a methods-and-software paper with controlled diagnostic studies. More tests and more successful notebook runs improve the artifact; they do not establish external validity. A broader empirical claim would need independently selected faults from other implementations, a direct comparison with relevant framework tests, and documented repairs on additional real tasks. The existing AirPassengers example supports one known repair on one series. The selected implementation regressions and covariance conditions remain selected cases even when their evaluation seeds are new.

The completed supplement sharpens the argument: different declared relations miss different regressions, and restoring the specified recursive algorithm can worsen average forecasting error in a random-walk condition. The covariance study also exposes the loss of detection power alongside fewer clean suite alarms after multiplicity adjustment. These counterexamples make the verification claim more informative than a list of successful detections. Keep them prominent in the abstract and discussion; do not turn agreement with one reference implementation into a claim of predictive superiority.

Prepare a reviewable submission candidate by pinning an immutable source revision, reproducing both study protocols and the generated manuscript, recording its environment, and having the author review the argument, bibliography, figures, and code. Choose a venue for the resulting contribution and its evidence. A methods or research-software venue can assess the integrated workflow; broad claims about debugging effectiveness require the additional independent evaluation above. Venue selection, release, and submission remain separate author decisions. The current experiments use local CPU resources.

The manuscript revises four earlier positions: treating a noise-free generator component as an optimal forecast without specifying the loss and available information; fresh sampling as a solution to multiple testing; synthetic success as evidence of general real-data reliability; and priority for forecasting testing itself. The closest reviewed predecessors are [Dwarakanath et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1109/MET.2019.00014), [SynTSBench (NeurIPS 2025)](https://doi.org/10.52202/085713-4860), [SimpleTimeBench (October 2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02058v1), [FinStressTS (KDD 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817578), and [FinTSB (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-026-51064-5). The univariate and multivariate financial process families, including jumps, hidden regimes, and CCC-GARCH, are software capabilities and illustrative reference demonstrations; they add no cases to the two recorded empirical studies.
