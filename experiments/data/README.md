# AirPassengers data provenance

`AirPassengers.csv` is the 144-month Box–Jenkins international airline passenger
series, January 1949–December 1960, in thousands of passengers. The columns are
the original Rdatasets CSV columns; the study constructs a monthly PeriodIndex
from January 1949 rather than rounding fractional years.

- Authoritative description: https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/datasets/html/AirPassengers.html
- Downloaded CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincentarelbundock/Rdatasets/master/csv/datasets/AirPassengers.csv
- Retrieved: 24 October 2026.
- SHA-256: `bdb98adbd418a6de6842a742e0602f363c3b841c26677e7e61a1e055e9509bd8`
- Original bibliographic source: Box, Jenkins and Reinsel (1994), *Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control*, third edition.
- R datasets licensing information: https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/datasets/DESCRIPTION
- Rdatasets distribution information: https://github.com/vincentarelbundock/Rdatasets

The data are used solely for one transparent implementation-repair illustration.
The four origins are observations from one series, not four independent datasets.
The numerical observations are not authored by TimeGym. Retain this source record
when redistributing the research artifact.
